# Innovation Labs for Schools in India

School innovation labs introduce students to practical technology education beyond traditional classroom learning.

Students can explore robotics, Artificial Intelligence, coding, electronics, and STEM-based activities.

Shard Center for Innovation provides information about school onboarding and technology-based learning opportunities.

Explore: https://scilindia.org/school-onboarding

## Learning Benefits

- Practical understanding of technology
- Creative thinking and experimentation
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Logical reasoning and problem-solving
- Exposure to emerging technologies
